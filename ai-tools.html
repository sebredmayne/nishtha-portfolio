Mosaic Pulse

A scraper reads through what people complain about on Reddit and across the internet. Pulse finds the complaints worth solving and turns them into product ideas, with who it's for, the format and a price band.

  2. 02Scrapecomplaints from Reddit, reviews and Google Trends
  3. 03Maphow painful vs how crowded each problem is
  4. 04Recommendscored product ideas, ready to decide on
CHOICES I MADE
→ Complaints over guesses: start from what people actually say, not what a brand assumes they want.
→ Two kinds of gap: painful and empty is a blue ocean; painful and crowded means make the existing product better.
→ Decision-ready: every idea is a card with who it's for, the gap, format and price band.
